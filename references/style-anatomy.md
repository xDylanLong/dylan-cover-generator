# Style anatomy

The supplied examples are 390×520 screenshots. Their lower-left heart and number are platform overlays, not cover content. The bundled references are cropped from the top of the screenshots to remove that overlay.

## Shared language

- Vertical, high-contrast thumbnail with a near-black/navy foundation.
- The headline is the first visual object: oversized, compact, mostly Chinese with occasional English tool names, and usually white/red/cyan/green.
- Text has thick shadow, outline, extrusion, tilt, or perspective. It behaves like a physical object rather than a quiet caption.
- A human figure occupies the middle or lower layer with an expressive face and a readable hand gesture.
- Topic evidence is literal and three-dimensional: papers, apps, screens, cards, robots, devices, or a result grid.
- Lighting is cinematic and directional: cool blue surroundings with red, magenta, or warm edge light; use particles, smoke, cracks, or radial motion for energy.

## Selection heuristics

Use `style-01` when the promise is combative or transformational; make the subject break through a barrier or explosion.

Use `style-02` when the story is mastery or technical skill; simplify the scene and let one tool cue support the title.

Use `style-03` for a question or choice; arrange floating windows around a thoughtful central character.

Use `style-04` for a direct comparison; put two opposing objects on either side of the character and keep the visual weights balanced.

Use `style-05` for a tutorial; push a device, panel, or labeled cards toward the viewer to create a hand-off.

Use `style-06` for a reveal or result; place the person to one side and let a screen, grid, or before/after board carry the evidence.

## Layout checks

- Headline: top 28–38%; no face behind the main letters.
- Character: center, lower center, or lower-left depending on archetype; eyes and gesture must remain readable.
- Props: 2–5 meaningful objects; avoid decorative clutter that competes with the title.
- Bottom edge: reserve a clean visual edge; never recreate social overlay UI.
- Thumbnail test: at 25% scale, the title, face/gesture, and topic object should still be identifiable.
